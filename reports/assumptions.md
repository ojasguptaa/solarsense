# Assumptions

_Generated by the pipeline — do not edit by hand._

Anything we could not measure directly is listed here, with its value and why we chose it.
In code these are marked `ASSUMPTION`; in the dashboard they get an ⓘ note.

| Assumption | Value | Why / how it was chosen | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Electricity tariff used to convert lost kWh into ₹ | 3.0 ₹/kWh | Assumed value, configurable as TARIFF_INR_PER_KWH in engine/config.py (re-run the pipeline after changing it). Shown on the website as "assumed tariff — see About". | Rounded value between recent Indian solar tariffs: SECI 2 GW solar + 1 GW/4 GWh storage tender, lowest bid ₹2.86/kWh ([Power Peak Digest, 17 Oct 2025](https://powerpeakdigest.com/seci-auction-discovers-lowest-tariff-for-2-gw-solar-with-1-gw-4-gwh-storage/)) and SECI rooftop solar auction, ₹3.34–4.65/kWh ([Indian Infrastructure, 17 Dec 2025](https://indianinfrastructure.com/2025/12/17/seci-announces-auction-results-for-6-25-mw-rooftop-solar-auction/)). |
| Plant 1 DC_POWER divided by a scale factor | 10.001 × | In daylight Plant 1 AC÷DC was 0.0979 (physically impossible) vs 0.9787 for Plant 2. We assume the inverters are similar and the DC sensor/logging was ~10.001× too large. | Computed from the data (engine/clean_data.py) |
| Share of inverter-days Isolation Forest treats as unusual | 5.0 % | Isolation Forest needs an expected share of odd days; we have no labels to measure it. Used only for the optional 'unusual day' hint, never for alerts. | Chosen by the team (engine/config.py) |
| Projected annual loss if faults are not fixed | 365 days scaled from the observed period | Estimate: 34-day loss scaled to 365 days, assuming the rest of the year behaves like May–June and nothing is fixed. Real yearly sunlight and fault patterns will differ. | Simple extrapolation (engine/losses.py) |
| Clear-sky sunlight learned from our own data | 95th percentile per 15-min time of day | No cloud sensor and no plant GPS location, so we cannot use a physical sun model. For each time of day we take the 95th percentile of sunlight over all 34 days (smoothed over 5 readings) as 'what a clear sky gives'. If a time of day was cloudy on almost every day, the reference there would be too low. | Computed from the data (engine/weather.py) |
| Heat loss = power a 25 °C panel would make − expected power | -0.392 / -0.304 %/°C (Plant 1 / Plant 2, measured) | Panels are rated at 25 °C. Each plant's temperature coefficient is measured from healthy, sunny rows (efficiency vs panel temperature, straight-line fit). Hot panels and strong sun happen together, so the coefficient is an estimate. If a fit is ever outside the believable range [-0.8, -0.15] %/°C, the typical -0.4 %/°C is used. | Computed from the data (engine/weather_temperature.py); typical range from silicon panel datasheets |
