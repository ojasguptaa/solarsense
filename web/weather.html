Weather & Energy

Sunlight vs a clear sky

The coloured band shows the sky every 15 minutes. Grey shading = sunlight blocked by clouds (gap between the clear-sky curve and real sunlight).

Where did the energy go?

From what a clear sky could give (panels at 25 °C) down to the energy actually made. Grey/orange = natural (nobody can fix clouds or heat), red = fixable faults.

Air and panel temperature

Panels get much hotter than the air in the sun. Shaded = hot hours (panel at or above 50 °C).
